Start the body with your actual opening paragraph — the `intro` field above
is the standfirst shown under the headline and used for social previews, so
don't just repeat it verbatim here; use the body to actually get into the
argument.

## Use subheads like this to break up a longer piece

Plain markdown — `##` for a major section, `###` for a sub-point within one.
Both are styled automatically.

A chart or supporting image goes on its own line, as a normal markdown
image, with an *italicised line immediately after it* treated as its
caption:

![Example chart — replace with a real one](/images/press-club-bangalore.jpg)
*Source: cite where the data/chart came from here.*

### Hyperlinking

Link out to AVI's own evidence wherever a claim needs backing — e.g. the
[law on possession](/india/law), the [evidence library](/science), or a
specific [litigation](/litigation) AVI has run. Link external sources the
normal markdown way too: [a study](https://example.com).

> A blockquote, e.g. for pulling out a key line from a judgment, a study,
> or an official statement.

Finish with AVI's actual position/takeaway — this is commentary, not just
a recap, so the piece should end with what AVI thinks should happen next.
