Before the 2019 national ordinance, several Indian states — Karnataka among
them — moved on their own to restrict or ban e-cigarette sale. AVI delegates
responded city by city, holding press conferences to walk local media
through the research and case studies the state decisions had not
accounted for, arguing the same case made nationally: that regulation, not
prohibition, was the evidence-based path, and that state bans would push
existing users toward an unregulated market rather than reduce use.
