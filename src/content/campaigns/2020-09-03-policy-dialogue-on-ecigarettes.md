Roughly a year into the national ENDS ban, AVI convened a public policy
dialogue in New Delhi to bring together the range of expertise the original
ban decision had left out: Samrat Chowdhery (AVI director, who
had become president of the International Network of Nicotine Consumer
Organisations' (INNCO) governing board two months earlier) on policy and
consumer advocacy; Dr Rohan Sequeira, a cardio-metabolic
physician at Mumbai's Jaslok Hospital, on the clinical case for tobacco harm
reduction; Dr Farrukh Khan, a senior Delhi High Court lawyer, on the pending
legal challenges; and Kanav Rishi Kumar, an ex-smoker who had switched to
vaping, speaking to lived experience.

The event drew press coverage and framed the questions AVI would keep
raising for years afterward: what evidence, if any, justified the ban, and
what had actually happened to smokers and vapers in the year since it took
effect.
