Every May 30, vapers and ex-smokers around the world mark World Vape Day —
a coordinated push by consumer advocates to put lived quit-and-switch
stories in front of the public and policymakers on the same day, in one
voice. AVI ran India's participation as a multi-part campaign: a Twitter
push tying into the global effort, an evening livestream where vapers spoke
for a few minutes each about their own quit journeys, a social-media
sensitisation drive aimed at the Indian public, and a ready-made email
template supporters could send straight to their MPs and MLAs.

The campaign leaned on the same core argument each year: with an estimated
42 million vapers worldwide standing behind harm reduction, and roughly 11
crore Indian smokers who could benefit from access to safer alternatives,
the goal was to make that constituency impossible to ignore.
