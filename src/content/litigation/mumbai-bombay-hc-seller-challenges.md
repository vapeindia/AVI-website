Both matters are **seller-brought cases, clearly distinct from AVI**: neither was
filed, funded, or organised by AVI, and AVI was not a party to either. AVI is a
consumer advocacy organisation and deliberately does not fund, file, or direct
litigation on behalf of vendors or manufacturers — doing so would blur the line
between consumer advocacy and industry action. Where these cases drew on research
or legal arguments AVI has separately developed and published, that reflects
publicly available analysis, not coordination between AVI and the litigants.
