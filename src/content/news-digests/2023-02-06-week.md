summary: "India's tobacco control landscape faced competing pressures this week. Enforcement authorities continued routine operations against illicit tobacco trafficking, including an arrest in Thane for unauthorized distribution. Meanwhile, public health researchers highlighted a significant policy gap, pointing out that bidis face substantially lower taxation than other tobacco products despite comparable health risks. Experts contended that the current tax disparity—with bidis taxed at roughly 22% compared to over 50% for cigarettes and smokeless tobacco—may inadvertently guide consumers toward cheaper alternatives rather than supporting harm-reduction goals."
