Simultaneously, regulatory developments abroad reflected ongoing debates about nicotine-containing products: the EU launched a public consultation on its tobacco directive governing e-cigarettes, Malaysia grappled with how generational endgame policies might address vaping, and US advocates pushed narrower retail-focused enforcement strategies for flavored products rather than broader restrictions. These parallel discussions underscore differing philosophies on whether restrictive bans or regulated alternatives better serve public health objectives."
