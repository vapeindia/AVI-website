Coverage of AVI's RTI finding that Karnataka's 2015 ENDS ban circular was
issued without any supporting study, quoting the Union health ministry's
Tobacco Control Division reply. Names Praveen Vijayan Pillai as the
IT professional who filed the RTI, and oncologist Dr Vishal Rao (Karnataka
Anti-Tobacco Cell) as citing studies for the ban — the same RTI reply this
site's RTI Replies page and the Karnataka litigation entry draw on.
