Early independent interview with Samrat Chowdhery, describing AVI (founded
2016, then ~500 registered members) and its effort to head off a threatened
nationwide e-cigarette ban. Chowdhery frames vaping as a harm-reduction tool
for India's smokers and argues public opinion will shift as more vapers
share their own quit stories — the same reasoning behind this site's
testimonials section.
