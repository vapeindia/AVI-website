date: 2018-07-13
outlet: "AVI press release"
type: "press-release"
archivePdf: "/archive/press/2018-chennai-tn-opposes-ban.docx"
---

AVI met Tamil Nadu health minister Dr C Vijaya Baskar and principal health secretary Dr J
Radhakrishnan in Chennai to oppose the state's proposed e-cigarette ban, urging regulation
instead. AVI presented over 4,000 pages of research and offered technical help in framing a
state vaping policy. Director Samrat Chowdhery and director Pratik Gupta are quoted citing
endorsements from the Royal College of Physicians, Public Health England and other bodies
for the relative safety of e-cigarettes.
