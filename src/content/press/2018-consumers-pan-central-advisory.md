AVI's first reaction to the Union Health Ministry's 28 August 2018 advisory urging states to
ban e-cigarettes, questioning why tobacco cigarettes weren't targeted instead, and disputing
the claim that vaping increases teen smoking by citing a 60,000-teen Public Health England
survey. Samrat Chowdhery also contests claims about heavy-metal content in e-vapour and
notes the government cited only the 30 nations that have banned ENDS while omitting the 65
that regulate them.
