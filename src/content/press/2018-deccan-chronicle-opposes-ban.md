National coverage of AVI's position against an outright ban then under
consideration, quoting director Samrat Chowdhery's warning that banning
e-cigarettes "without collecting sufficient peer reviewed indigenous
research data will put public health in India at greater risk" for the
country's 100 million-plus smokers.
