AVI's release on a Delhi High Court hearing in the Seema Sehgal PIL (see this
site's Litigation page), reporting the central government's disclosure in court
that it intended to ban e-cigarettes despite the petitioner having asked only for
regulation — with the bench remarking it seemed odd to ban e-cigarettes while
leaving tobacco cigarettes unbanned. Quotes AVI director Samrat Chowdhery
committing to contest the ban.
