title: "HC asks govt to hear e-cigarette traders"
date: 2018-08-21
outlet: "AVI press release (via Brands2Life)"
type: "press-release"
---

AVI's contemporaneous report on the Delhi High Court's 21 August 2018 order
in the Seema Sehgal PIL, directly corroborating this site's Litigation page:
the court directed the health ministry to hear e-cigarette traders and submit
a regulatory plan within a week. Quotes Samrat Chowdhery welcoming the order
and calling for risk-proportionate tax rates rather than a ban.
