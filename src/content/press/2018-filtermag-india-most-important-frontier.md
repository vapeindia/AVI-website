---

Argues that India, with an estimated 270 million tobacco users, needs to
embrace harm reduction — safer alternatives like e-cigarettes and
smokeless nicotine products — rather than relying solely on cold-turkey
cessation and taxation, which the piece says have failed to meaningfully
cut tobacco use or deaths. Names India's tobacco-control establishment and
state economic interests in the tobacco industry as the main obstacles to
that shift.
