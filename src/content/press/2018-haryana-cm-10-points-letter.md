---
title: "AVI's 10-point letter to the Haryana Chief Minister on ENDS policy"
date: 2018-07-24
outlet: "AVI letter to Haryana Chief Minister Manohar Lal Khattar"
type: "press-release"
---

A formal representation, not a press release in the usual sense but a
direct letter, asking Haryana's government to weigh the evidence before
finalising ENDS policy: cites Royal College of Physicians (UK), Public
Health England, and other bodies on relative risk, the tar-not-nicotine
distinction, comparative cessation rates (NRT vs. vaping), and the
industry's economic potential. Welcomes regulation of e-cigarettes under
COTPA rather than a ban. Distinct from and earlier than this site's
existing Haryana entry (Jan 2019, warning that a ban could harm public
health) — this is the original policy ask, seven months prior. **Note:**
the source document's body text has a copy-paste artifact — it asks
Haryana's CM to consider AVI's points "before finalising a policy," but one
sentence refers to "the TN Government," suggesting this letter reused
wording from a Tamil Nadu-directed template. Not previously on the site.
