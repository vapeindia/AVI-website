---
title: "AVI's 10-point letter to the Haryana Chief Minister on ENDS policy"
date: 2018-07-24
outlet: "AVI letter to Haryana Chief Minister Manohar Lal Khattar"
type: "press-release"
---

A formal letter, not a press release in the usual sense, asking Haryana's
government to weigh the evidence — citing the Royal College of Physicians
(UK), Public Health England and other bodies on relative risk and
comparative cessation rates — before finalising ENDS policy, and to
regulate e-cigarettes under COTPA rather than ban them outright.
