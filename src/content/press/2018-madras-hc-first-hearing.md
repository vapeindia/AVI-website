Direct corroboration for this site's Chennai litigation entry: reports the
Madras High Court's first hearing, with advocate Vivek Menon arguing the ban
violated Article 14, the state given three weeks to reply, and petitioner
Cary Edwards quoted by name. Also notes that, the same day, the Delhi High
Court ruled the central advisory recommending an ENDS ban was "not binding on
states." Quotes AVI advocate Pingal Khan, "who has challenged the vape ban in
neighbouring Karnataka."
