Release from a New Delhi policy conference featuring international THR
figures: Prof. David Sweanor (University of Ottawa), Dr Alex Wodak (former
president, International Harm Reduction Association), Dr Konstantinos
Farsalinos, and NEHU's Prof. Rajesh Sharan — the same academic AVI had cited
in earlier releases. One of the earliest instances of AVI convening
international harm-reduction experts in India.
