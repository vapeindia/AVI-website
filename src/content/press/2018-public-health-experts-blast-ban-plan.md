A week after the Centre's advisory, AVI gathered reactions from independent public health
experts rather than its own directors: Prof Rajesh Sharan (biochemistry, North Eastern Hill
University) questioned why e-cigarettes were being targeted while tobacco cigarettes weren't;
Mumbai cardio-metabolic physician Dr Rohan Sequeira called the advisory a "knee-jerk
reaction" citing the UK's reversal of its own earlier vaping restrictions; and Mumbai dental
surgeon Dr Anand Krishnamurthy described his own improved health after switching from
smoking to vaping.
