Joint HRPR/AVI release opposing Punjab's ENDS ban, issued from Chandigarh. Notes
Punjab's own tobacco-use rate rose from 11.7% (2010) to 13.4% (2017) despite
existing controls, and quotes Mumbai physician Dr Rohan Sequeira and Delhi
advocate Dr Farrukh Khan, who offered pro bono legal services to challenge state
ENDS bans. States HRPR had by this point "challenged ENDS bans in two states,
Karnataka and Jammu & Kashmir, with a legal challenge in Tamil Nadu about to be
filed" — corroborating the timeline on this site's Litigation page.
