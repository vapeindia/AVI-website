Explainer release distinguishing nicotine (addictive but not the primary
cause of smoking-related disease) from tar and combustion byproducts, quoting
Samrat Chowdhery and tobacco-harm-reduction pioneer Michael Russell — "people
smoke for nicotine but they die from the tar." One of AVI's earliest public
statements, issued as states began banning ENDS.
