---
title: "AVI appeals to e-cigarette users to urge government to repeal the vape ban"
date: 2019-11-04
outlet: "AVI press release (via Brands2Life)"
type: "press-release"
---

Call to action urging vapers to write to the health ministry opposing the
ENDS ban ordinance, citing British Parliament criticism that India's ban was
"not based on evidence." Quotes Samrat Chowdhery on cessation success rates
(willpower ~5%, nicotine gum ~7%) as the case against removing e-cigarettes
as a quit option.
