An early reaction to reports the government would bring an ordinance banning e-cigarettes,
ahead of the ordinance's actual promulgation later that month — distinct from AVI's later
"regressive" response after the ordinance passed. AVI questioned the government's impartiality
given its 28% stake in ITC, calling the move an attempt to bypass the judiciary after both Delhi
and Bombay High Courts had already stayed state-level vape bans. Quotes Samrat Chowdhery,
Delhi HC lawyer Farrukh Khan and cardio-metabolic physician Dr Rohan Sequeira.
