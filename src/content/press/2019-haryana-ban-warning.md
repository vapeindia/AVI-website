Response to an unnamed NGO's letter urging Haryana CM Manohar Lal Khattar to
ban e-cigarettes "without any facts and substantial study." Cites the state's
19.7% smoking rate (GATS 2016-17) and offers AVI's research to help frame
regulation instead. **Date is approximate** — the source carries no dateline;
estimated from citation style consistent with other releases from this
period.
