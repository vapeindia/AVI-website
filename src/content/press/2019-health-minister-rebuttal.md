AVI's point-by-point rebuttal of Union Health Minister Dr Harsh Vardhan's
editorial defending the national ENDS ban: disputes his tobacco-decline
statistics against US/UK comparators, challenges the teen-uptake "gateway"
claim by citing a 60,000-teen UK study, and separates the US vaping-related
lung-injury (EVALI) deaths — caused by illicit cannabis-oil vapes — from legal
nicotine vaping. Quotes Samrat Chowdhery, Jagannath Sarangapani and Dhaval
Gogate.
