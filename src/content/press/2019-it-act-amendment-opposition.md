Opposed a proposed IT Act amendment that would have banned publishing any
information about ENDS online, framed as part of a fake-news crackdown.
Quotes NEHU professor Dr R N Sharan on the public-health cost of information
gags, and advocate Dr Farrukh Khan on the amendment's constitutionality —
noting it would follow a pattern of departments (Customs, now IT) relying on
an advisory a Delhi High Court judgment had already called non-binding on
states.
