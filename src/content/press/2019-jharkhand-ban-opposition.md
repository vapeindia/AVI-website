---
title: "AVI opposes Jharkhand government proposal to ban e-cigarettes"
date: 2019-03-15
outlet: "AVI press release (via Brands2Life)"
type: "press-release"
---

Response to Jharkhand health secretary Dr Nitin Madan Kulkarni's 7 March 2019
ban announcement, urging regulation instead. States AVI wrote to the health
minister and secretary requesting an appointment and offered 120 research
papers (4,000+ pages) toward a regulatory policy, and separately offered the
state technical help drafting one.
