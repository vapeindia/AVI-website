---
title: "Consumers demand Karnataka government lift ban on e-cigarettes"
date: 2019-05-10
outlet: "AVI press release (via Brands2Life)"
type: "press-release"
---

Released from Bengaluru, timed to the Karnataka litigation on this site's
Litigation page. States plainly that "the organisation filed public interest
litigation (PIL) in the Karnataka High Court" against the state's ENDS ban,
quotes Samrat Chowdhery and advocate Pingal Khan of Ashlar Law ("representing
AVI in court"), plus consumers Karthik Krishnan and Pradeep Herle on their
experience switching from smoking to vaping.
