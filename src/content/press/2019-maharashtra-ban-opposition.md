date: 2019-05-31
outlet: "AVI press release (via Brands2Life)"

Released on World No Tobacco Day, opposing the Maharashtra FDA's ban on ENDS
manufacture, distribution and sale. Quotes Samrat Chowdhery, a Jaslok Hospital
physician, and advocate Wesley Menezes, who noted the Delhi High Court had
already stayed a similar ban and suggested Maharashtra's could face the same
legal challenge — the seller-brought Bombay High Court cases now on this
site's Litigation page followed a few weeks later.
