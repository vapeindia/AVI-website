---
title: "International experts gather in Mumbai for conference on reducing tobacco-related harms"
date: 2019-10-13
outlet: "AVI press release (via Brands2Life)"
type: "press-release"
---

A tobacco harm reduction conference jointly hosted by AVI and Knowledge-Action-
Change (KAC), the UK public health consultancy behind the Global State of
Tobacco Harm Reduction report. Panel included KAC's Kevin Molloy, Tagore
Medical College's Dr Sree Sucharitha, New Nicotine Alliance UK's Jessica
Harding, AVI's Samrat Chowdhery and Jagannath Sarangapani, and advocate Pingal
Khan.
