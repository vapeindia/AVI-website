title: "AVI calls for nationwide protest against the e-cigarette ban ordinance"
date: 2019-09-26
outlet: "AVI press release (via Brands2Life)"

---

Announces a nationwide call for vapers to protest the ban ordinance in
multiple cities on September 28, 2019, coordinated by Jagannath Sarangapani
(described as the protests' national coordinator). Samrat Chowdhery is
quoted condemning the ordinance and citing over 11 crore Indian smokers
denied access to harm reduction alternatives. **The source document names
a city/venue inconsistently** — its title references Hyderabad's Lamakaan
venue, but the body text (evidently reused from a template) describes
Bangalore vapers assembling at Mumbai's Marathi Patrakar Sangh — so this
entry deliberately doesn't pin down a specific single city or venue. Not
previously on the site.
