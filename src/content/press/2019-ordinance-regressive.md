AVI's response to the ENDS Ordinance (PECA's immediate predecessor), calling
it "a black day" and alleging the government's 28% stake in ITC as a conflict
of interest. Quotes Samrat Chowdhery, Diwan Advocates partner Dr Farrukh Khan
("ultra vires to the constitutional framework"), pulmonologist Dr Bharat
Gopal, and consumer Kanav Rishi Kumar. Notes 3,300 users had already written
to the Prime Minister opposing the ban.
