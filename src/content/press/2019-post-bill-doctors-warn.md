Bill, 2019, gathering physician reaction. Quotes pulmonologist Dr Bharat

the ban as one-sided), Dr Aparajeet Kar, and AVI director Jagannath
Sarangapani. **Date is approximate** — estimated from the bill's early
December 2019 passage.
