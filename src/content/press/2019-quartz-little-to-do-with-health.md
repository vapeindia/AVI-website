Quartz analysis (Niharika Sharma) arguing the ban's real driver was protectionism rather than
health. Quotes Samrat Chowdhery, founder of AVI, on the government's financial stake in
cigarette maker ITC: "The government owns 28% of ITC, a leading manufacturer of cigarettes,
which means it is directly profiteering from the cigarette trade, along with earning
thousands of crores in taxes on cigarettes."
