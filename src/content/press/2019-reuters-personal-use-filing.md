Reuters report (Aditya Kalra) on a Kolkata court filing in which the Health Ministry argued
personal use of e-cigarettes was implicitly banned alongside sale and manufacture, despite
earlier official assurances to the contrary. Quotes Samrat Chowdhery of AVI: "This filing goes
against the Health Ministry's announcements that personal use is not banned" — he argued the
ban order should carry an explicit personal-use exclusion.
