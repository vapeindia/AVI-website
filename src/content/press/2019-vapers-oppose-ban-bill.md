---
title: "Vapers write enmasse to oppose vape ban bill"
date: 2019-10-11
outlet: "AVI press release (via Brands2Life)"
type: "press-release"
---

AVI publicised that over 2,000 e-cigarette users submitted responses opposing
the Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Bill, 2019 during its public-comment
period, arguing the bill relied on an outdated 2014 WHO report superseded by
more recent guidance favouring regulation over prohibition, and noting tobacco
company share prices rose after the ban was announced.
