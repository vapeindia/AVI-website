date: 2020-01-17
outlet: "AVI press release (via Brands2Life)"
type: "press-release"
---

Reports confiscations at Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Delhi and
Bhavnagar airports following BCAS's 10 January 2020 circular, and states AVI
was "mulling legal options" — the direct run-up to the flight-ban litigation
now on this site's Litigation page. Quotes an affected traveller, Dhruva
Kudva (Bangalore, 16 Jan confiscation), and advocate Pingal Khan on the
circular's misreading of the law.
