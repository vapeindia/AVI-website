AVI's first reaction to BCAS's 10 January 2020 circular confiscating
e-cigarettes at airports, arguing the aviation regulator misread the law —
personal use was excluded from the ban per the Health Minister's own Lok
Sabha assurance — and that AVI was "seeking legal assistance." Predates the
more detailed 3 March 2020 release once the international-traveller fallout
became clear. **Date is approximate**: the source document carries no
dateline, only internal references to the 10 Jan 2020 circular as recent.
