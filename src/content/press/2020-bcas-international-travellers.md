AVI press release on the fallout from BCAS's January 2020 circular confiscating
e-cigarettes at Indian airports: cited UK charity New Nicotine Alliance's letter
to the UK government over confiscations from British tourists, estimated over
110,000 US and 50,000 UK visitors affected annually, and quoted AVI board member
Maneesh Kasera and advocate Pingal Khan — who said AVI was "looking to challenge
it in court," the origin of the flight-ban litigation now on this site's
Litigation page.
