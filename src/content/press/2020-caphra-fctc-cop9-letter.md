---
title: "Big coalition urges India's health ministry to discuss rights of vapers in a global treaty"
date: 2020-03-12
outlet: "CAPHRA release (via Brands2Life, AVI's PR agency)"
type: "press-release"
---

Not an AVI release directly, but distributed through AVI's own PR agency
(Brands2Life) as a member of the Coalition of Asia Pacific Tobacco Harm
Reduction Advocates (CAPHRA): urged India's health ministry to raise vapers'
rights ahead of the WHO-FCTC's COP9 session, and criticised the treaty body
for 18 years of inaction on harm reduction.
