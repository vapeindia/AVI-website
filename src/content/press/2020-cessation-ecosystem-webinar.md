Reports on an HRPR-hosted webinar where panelists from Kolkata, Delhi,
Hyderabad, Indore and Pune shared personal experiences of how hard
quitting smoking unaided ("cold turkey") really is, and argued for a more
robust cessation ecosystem — including wider access to nicotine
replacement and vaping — pointing out that India's ENDS ban leaves
smokers fewer cessation routes than the UK, US or Canada.
