**Date is approximate** — the source dateline reads "New Delhi, August ___,
2020" with the day left blank in the draft. Reports on a webinar, hosted by
Harm Reduction Policy & Research (HRPR) — referred to by its former name in
the source document, corrected here per the site-wide HRPR/CHRA
substitution (see CLAUDE.md) — assessing why quitting smoking unaided
("cold turkey") is hard. Panelists from Kolkata, Delhi, Hyderabad, Indore
and Pune shared personal quitting experiences; tobacco cessation activist
Maneesh Kasera and Delhi-based host Kanav Kumar argued for expanding
cessation options, including nicotine replacement and vaping, and Kumar
specifically criticised India's ENDS ban as leaving smokers with fewer
cessation routes than the UK, US or Canada. Not previously on the site.
