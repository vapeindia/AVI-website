On-camera segment for DW's German-language news-video series examining whether e-cigarettes
are healthier or more dangerous than smoking, alongside an oncologist, a cancer-research
scientist and a German vape-trade representative. Samrat Chowdhery appears on camera,
captioned "Gründer vom Verband der Vaper Indien" (founder of the Association of Vapers
India), describing in German how vaping helped him quit smoking and regain his fitness,
health and sense of taste.
