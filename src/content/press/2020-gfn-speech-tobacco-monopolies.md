Recap of Samrat Chowdhery's address to the (virtual) Global Forum on
Nicotine, arguing state ownership stakes in tobacco monopolies — India's
government holds nearly a third of ITC — create a structural conflict of
interest in tobacco policy, and calling for the state to divest from the
industry.
