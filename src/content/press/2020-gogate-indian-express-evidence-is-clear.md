A separate, first-person Dhaval Gogate op-ed draft for The Indian Express — distinct from his
other Indian Express piece, "Vaping: The way forward for tobacco harm reduction" (also in this
collection) — recounting his own switch from smoking to vaping and making the evidence case
that nicotine itself is not what harms smokers, tar and combustion are. **Date is approximate**,
matched to the companion piece from the same source batch — the archived draft carries no
dateline, and independent confirmation this exact draft (as opposed to a further-edited version)
ran in print has not been done.
