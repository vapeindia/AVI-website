---
title: "Policy reforms needed to combat India's tobacco crisis: Global report"
date: 2020-11-05
outlet: "AVI press release (via Brands2Life)"
type: "press-release"
---

Covered the UK launch of Knowledge Action Change's biennial "Burning Issues"
Global State of Tobacco Harm Reduction report, which named India among four
countries that had banned THR products since 2018 (against 22 that had
regulated or reversed bans), and noted the government's 28% ITC stake
alongside its ban decision. Quotes Samrat Chowdhery in his INNCO president
and AVI director capacity.
