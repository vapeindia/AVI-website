Extended statement by Samrat Chowdhery (HRPR director) on the ordinance's
first anniversary: argues the ban failed its own stated goal of protecting
youth, since black-market access removed the checks regulation would have
provided, and cites New Zealand, the Philippines and Russia moving toward
regulation in the intervening year.
