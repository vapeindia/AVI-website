Kasera, described as "an IT professional and
a tobacco harm reduction advocate," argues for pragmatic harm reduction
(snus, e-cigarettes) over abstinence-only policy, cites the UK/Sweden
experience, and calls for government disinvestment from tobacco businesses
and redirecting tobacco tax revenue to cessation infrastructure.
