Advance notice of the 18 September 2020 "Black Day" protest marking one year since the ENDS
ordinance, held simultaneously in Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Kolkata plus an
online rally. Distinct from the quote compilations AVI issued on the day itself (and the recap
issued afterward) — this is the earlier call-to-action release. Notes AVI had also written to
every Member of Parliament setting out 10 reasons the ban had failed.
