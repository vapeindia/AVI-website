Announced Samrat Chowdhery's appointment as President of the International
Network of Nicotine Consumer Organisations (INNCO), a 34-country body with UN
observer status. Confirms he founded HRPR and directs AVI, that "HRPR and AVI
have been advocating for risk reduction in tobacco use since 2016," and that
he won GFN's Advocate of the Year award in 2018.

**Note (2026):** the original release named this organisation differently;
per site-owner instruction it is referred to here as Harm Reduction Policy
&amp; Research (HRPR) throughout, for consistency with how it's named
elsewhere on this site.
