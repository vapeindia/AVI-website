Opposed a position paper by Paris-based NGO "The Union" proposing developing
countries ban ENDS and heated tobacco on the grounds of weak regulation.
Quotes Samrat Chowdhery (identified here as HRPR director), researcher Dr
Sree Sucharitha, and advocate Pingal Khan, who called the reasoning
discriminatory and noted bans in Mexico and Thailand had "already created a
thriving black market."
