Six months after the ban, gathered accounts of vapers reporting a return to
smoking and worsening health, including pilot Kamal Mordani, who could no
longer carry his device under the BCAS flight restriction and returned to
smoking mid-career. Quotes Samrat Chowdhery on COPD symptoms reported by
members forced back to combustible cigarettes.
