A post-event recap of the 18 September 2020 "Black Day" nationwide protest, distinct from the
same day's two quote-sheet entries already on this site (one from Samrat Chowdhery alone, one
from a separate set of Indian vapers) — this release narrates the protest itself and carries its
own set of quotes: Chowdhery, pediatrician Dr Vikas Jain, Prof David Sweanor (Ottawa), Prof
Gerry Stimson (Imperial College London), legal counsel Pingal Khan, Kolkata vaper Kamal
Mordani, and Spanish consumer advocate Angeles Muntadas.
