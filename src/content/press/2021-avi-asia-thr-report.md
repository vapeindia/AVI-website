AVI co-hosted, with Knowledge Action Change, the launch of "Tobacco Harm
Reduction: A Burning Issue for Asia and the Far East," a regional report by
British author Harry Shapiro. Jagannath Sarangapani moderated; quotes Samrat
Chowdhery in his capacity as INNCO president. **Date is approximate** —
inferred from the report launch falling on a Sunday, 18 April, matching 2021.
