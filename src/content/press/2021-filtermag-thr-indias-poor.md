---
title: "Can Tobacco Harm Reduction Work for India's Poor?"
date: 2021-04-30
outlet: "Filter"
url: "https://filtermag.org/tobacco-harm-reduction-india-poor/"
type: "op-ed"
---

Argues that harm reduction — making lower-risk alternatives available and
affordable — matters specifically for India's poor, since conventional
control measures like tax increases push economically vulnerable users
toward cheaper, more dangerous products rather than addressing why tobacco
use is prevalent in these communities in the first place: nicotine helps
alleviate hunger for many living in poverty, and the cessation support
that exists (mostly phone counselling) has little reach into poor
communities, while NRTs remain unaffordable despite WHO essential-medicine
status.
