---
title: "Can Tobacco Harm Reduction Work for India's Poor?"
date: 2021-04-30
outlet: "Authored op-ed by Samrat Chowdhery, for Filter"
type: "op-ed"
---

Argues that harm reduction — making lower-risk alternatives available and
affordable — matters specifically for India's poor, since conventional
control measures like tax increases push economically vulnerable users
toward cheaper, more dangerous products rather than addressing why tobacco
use is prevalent in these communities in the first place. Found via
Filter's author index during a 2026-09-09 press-coverage recheck. Not
previously on the site. See
[Filter's author page](https://filtermag.org/author/samrat-chowdhery/)
for the original.
