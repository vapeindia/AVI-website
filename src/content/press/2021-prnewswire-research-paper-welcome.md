AVI endorsed a peer-reviewed paper co-authored by fifteen tobacco-control
scientists including Kenneth Warner, published in the American Journal of
Public Health, arguing for balanced e-cigarette policy that supports
smoking cessation while restricting youth access — and urged Indian
policymakers to weigh THR given the country's tobacco burden.
