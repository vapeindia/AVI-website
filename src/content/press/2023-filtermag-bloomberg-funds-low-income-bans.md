---
title: "Will New Bloomberg Funds Spread Vape Bans in Low-Income Countries?"
date: 2023-03-30
outlet: "Authored op-ed by Samrat Chowdhery, for Filter"
url: "https://filtermag.org/bloomberg-vape-bans-low-income-countries/"
type: "op-ed"
---

Argues that a $420 million Bloomberg Philanthropies tobacco-control
initiative risks funding blanket vaping bans in low- and middle-income
countries regardless of local conditions, despite evidence that vaping is
less harmful than smoking. Cites India, Mexico and the Philippines as
examples where the piece says Bloomberg-funded advocacy pushed
prohibitionist policy against the available evidence.
