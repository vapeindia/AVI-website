Not India-specific — Samrat Chowdhery writing in his INNCO-president
capacity on a peer market's policy. Covers Taiwan's move to ban nicotine
vaping products under amendments to its Tobacco Hazards Prevention Act,
with fines for violations. Argues the ban rests on misinformation about
vaping and prioritises international tobacco-control alignment over
evidence-based harm reduction for smokers.
