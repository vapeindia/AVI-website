India-specific and directly relevant to AVI's core advocacy. Details how
India's government went beyond its 2019 vaping ban to restrict research
and media coverage of tobacco harm reduction: a December 2023 directive
required doctors to get approval before researching vapes or heated
tobacco, and a May 2023 government order barred media from publishing
content seen as promoting e-cigarettes. Argues these measures suppressed
public debate on lower-risk alternatives for India's roughly 120 million
adult smokers.
