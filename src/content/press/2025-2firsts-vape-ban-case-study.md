Byline op-ed for the trade outlet 2Firsts marking five years of PECA,
arguing the ban produced a thriving illicit market, lost tobacco-tax
revenue, and eliminated jobs, while denying India's smokers access to
"substantially less harmful" alternatives — making the case for
regulation over prohibition.
