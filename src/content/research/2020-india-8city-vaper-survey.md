Found during a 2026-09-09 research pass into the evidence base available
for the site. Convenience-sample survey (self-selected vapers, not a
population-representative sample), so the 71.3% figure describes this
surveyed group, not Indian smokers generally — worth stating that caveat
wherever this is cited on the site. Full text on
[PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7106589/).
