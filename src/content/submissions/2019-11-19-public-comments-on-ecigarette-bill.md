---
title: "Public Comments on the E-Cigarette Bill — sent to every MP"
date: 2019-11-19
recipient: "All Members of Parliament, Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha"
type: "letter"
summary: "As Parliament prepared to debate the bill that ratified the ENDS ordinance into permanent law, AVI emailed a formal written submission — in both English and Hindi — individually to every sitting MP in both houses, asking them to read it before the debate."
---

With the Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Bill, 2019 (ratifying September's
ordinance into permanent law) due for debate, AVI did not limit its
advocacy to the health ministry or a handful of legislators — it wrote to
**every single Member of Parliament**, in both the Lok Sabha and Rajya
Sabha, individually, over several days in November 2019. Each email
carried the same short request in both English and Hindi:

> Dear Respected MP, the e-cigarette bill will be debated in this session
> of Parliament. We request you to review our attached submission so that
> an informed debate can take place before the bill is passed.

Two PDF submissions were attached to each email — one in English, one in
Hindi — making AVI's evidence-based case against the ban accessible to
legislators regardless of their working language. A number of these
emails bounced (several `sansad.nic.in` addresses were no longer valid,
per the delivery-failure notices in AVI's own outbox), but the bulk of
Parliament's membership was reached directly ahead of a vote that would
otherwise have proceeded without hearing the consumer side of the
argument at all.
