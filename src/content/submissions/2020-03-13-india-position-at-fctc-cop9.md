summary: "Ahead of the WHO FCTC's 9th Conference of Parties, AVI urged the Health Minister to withdraw the ENDS ban and take a harm-reduction stance into the negotiations, rather than let India's position be shaped without consumer input."
---

Sent directly to Dr Harsh Vardhan, then Union Health Minister, and the
Health Secretary, with a fuller letter enclosed in both English and Hindi
(not separately reproduced here). The covering email itself, reproduced
below in full, argued that traditional cessation methods alone cannot
address India's tobacco burden without a progressive stance on harm
reduction, and asked the Minister to carry that position into COP9, then
scheduled for November 2020 in the Netherlands (later postponed to
2021), and to withdraw the ban on sale, production, distribution,
advertising and transport of e-cigarettes at home.

**Full text of the covering email:**

Respected Sir/Madam,

India has the second largest smoking population in the world and suffers
over a million deaths annually which can be linked to tobacco related
illnesses.

In this connection and backed by overwhelming scientific evidence, we
firmly believe that India which ought to have been leading the way in
promoting tobacco cessation therapies and safer delivery systems like
e-cigarettes, snus and other THR (Tobacco Harm Reduction) therapies, has
made the grave mistake of banning their sale and distribution in India
while permitting them only for personal use. Less harmful alternatives
will reduce the huge public health burden from tobacco we currently face
in India.

Unfortunately, traditional tobacco cessation methods are insufficient to
tackle this burden unless the government takes a progressive stance on
tobacco harm reduction. Current public health policies are failing to
stop or discourage initiation of tobacco consumption.

We urge the government to give our enclosed letter (in Hindi and
English) a patient and clear reading and alter its position at COP9
which is to be held in the Netherlands in November 2020, and also to
withdraw the ban on sale, production, distribution, advertising and
transportation of e-cigarettes.

For the Association of Vapers India

Jagannath Sarangapani,
Board member - AVI
